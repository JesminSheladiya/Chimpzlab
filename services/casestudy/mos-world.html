MOS World

About Brand

MOS Utility Limited, known as MOS World, is a Mumbai-headquartered B2B2C digital fintech company offering banking, utility, travel, and e-commerce services through a nationwide network of agents and distributors.

The Mandate

ChimpzLab was engaged to reposition MOS World's corporate messaging and rebuild its brand identity ahead of the company's IPO.

The Challenge

MOS World's existing tagline and brand communications were not resonating effectively — with an impending IPO, precise corporate messaging was critical.

Clear, consistent corporate messaging was critical for engaging investors and internal stakeholders ahead of the IPO.

Our Solution

Brand Repositioning & Identity

We addressed MOS World's corporate communication challenges through audience research, design, and messaging strategy.

01

Audience Understanding

Through discussions and market research, we delved deep into understanding who MOS World's audience really was and what they sought in a brand.

02

Logo & Brand Philosophy

Based on the brand's core values and mission, we crafted a new logo that encapsulated the essence of the brand, rooted in a philosophy that served as the foundation for all future branding.

03

Tagline & Messaging

"Digital Bano, Tarakki Karo" was coined — a tagline that clearly communicates the brand's mission of empowering clients through digital transformation.

04

Brand Guidelines

Created a comprehensive brand guidelines document that serves as a blueprint for all future branding endeavors, ensuring consistency across all platforms.

The Impact

Ahead of an IPO, a brand's tagline is doing investor relations work whether it means to or not. "Digital Bano, Tarakki Karo" compressed the entire value proposition.

Refreshed
Identity

Refreshed brand identity that stands out and resonates with MOS World's clients.

Consistent
Branding

Consistent branding with guidelines and tone of voice ensuring authentic communication across all touchpoints.

Clear
Messaging

Clear tagline and messaging that make MOS World's value proposition easier for potential clients to understand.
