Soul Skin

About Brand

Soul Skin is a dermatology, cosmetology, skin and hair clinic based in Anna Nagar, Chennai, competing for patients in a dense, hyperlocal healthcare market.

The Mandate

Chimpzlab was engaged to win local search visibility for Soul Skin's core treatment categories within its own neighborhood.

The Challenge

For a single-location clinic, the entire battle is hyperlocal. Patients search by neighborhood, not by city, and every position lost to a competitor clinic is a patient who never calls.

Before the engagement, Soul Skin's core treatment terms sat two and three pages deep — well past where someone in discomfort or self-conscious about a skin condition would ever scroll.

Our Approach

Neighborhood-Level Precision

The plan focused on the neighborhood-level searches that actually convert to bookings, not city-wide terms that draw traffic without intent.

01

Hyperlocal Keyword Targeting

Built around Anna Nagar-specific searches for skin doctors, dermatologists and named treatments, rather than city-wide "Chennai" terms.

02

Treatment-Page Optimization

Dedicated pages for high-intent procedures like pigmentation treatment, laser hair removal and acne scar treatment, where searchers are close to booking.

03

AEO-Ready Content Structuring

Content built so the clinic can surface in AI-driven local search results, not only in traditional maps and listings.

04

Monthly Rank Tracking

Movement checked against a fixed set of neighborhood keywords to stay ahead as competitors adjust.

The Impact

For a single-location clinic there is no advertising budget wide enough to make up for being on page two. Local search rank is not the top of the funnel — it is the whole funnel.
